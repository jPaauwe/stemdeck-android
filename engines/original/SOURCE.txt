engine.cpp and engine.hpp are the StemDeck Android wrapper from builds 4–10
(decoding, resampling, loudness, WAV writing), with separate() replaced: it
hands the whole song to demucscpp::demucs_inference in one call, as
upstream's own command-line tool does. That separation runs on one thread and
holds the whole song in memory. Upstream picks its random time shift with
rand(); the wrapper seeds it with srand(1) so runs repeat.
